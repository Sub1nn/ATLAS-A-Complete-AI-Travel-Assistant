ATLAS Privacy Policy

Version: loading

ATLAS processes account details, travel preferences, conversations, uploaded files and technical request information to provide and secure the service.

Data and purposes

We use account and authentication data to provide accounts and prevent misuse; conversations and preferences to provide travel assistance and history; uploaded filenames, extracted text and document chunks to answer document questions; and request, error and usage data to operate, secure and improve reliability.

Providers and recipients

Depending on enabled features, data may be processed by the configured hosting and database providers, Groq for language generation, Google Maps Platform, OpenWeather, NewsAPI and Yelp for travel context, Pinecone for document search, Resend for account email, and the configured monitoring provider. Only data needed for the requested feature should be sent.

When Pinecone is enabled, ATLAS stores document identifiers, filenames and up to 3,000 characters of text per chunk as vector metadata. Integrated-index mode may additionally store up to 8,000 characters per chunk in its searchable text field. Do not upload unnecessary sensitive information.

International transfers

Transfer safeguard configured by the operator: loading. Provider locations and transfer terms should be reviewed by the operator before deployment.

Retention

Account data remains until deletion. Conversations, messages and documents follow the retention period selected in the app, between 30 and 730 days. Security and idempotency records use shorter operational expiry periods where configured.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, and may withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. The app provides export, retention and deletion controls. You may also complain to loading or your competent data-protection authority.

Automated processing

ATLAS generates travel assistance using automated systems. It is not intended to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. Verify safety, entry, health, legal, price and availability information with authoritative sources.

Security and deletion

Access uses short-lived tokens, secure refresh cookies and account controls. Account deletion is coordinated with document workers and remote vector deletion before local account records are removed. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
